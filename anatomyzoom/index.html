The picture zooms out – can you name the organ?

An anatomy learning game with 211 self-drawn structures in humans and dogs: organs and bones under the magnifier, “Where is it?” right on the body (with the distance in centimetres), Latin terms and human ↔ animal comparisons.

  • Journey of the Day through the body – blood, food or air – the same for everyone.
  • Organs in detail: eye, ear, heart, brain, tooth, skin, tongue, nose, kidney, hand and foot.
  • Spaced-repetition flashcards, a 3D body to rotate, daily streak and eight levels.

Currently in German and Azerbaijani, no account, works offline.

Not medical advice – for learning only. Free with ads, one-time “Ad-free” purchase. For learners aged 13 and up. All illustrations drawn by us. Developed with the help of AI (code, text, translations).
