Privacy Policy

for the app “Hen Heights” and this website · Last updated: November 19, 2026
This translation is provided for information. In case of doubt, the German version prevails.

In short

  • You don't need an account and don't have to sign up.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We don't run any servers for the app.
  • We don't analyze anything ourselves: no tracking, no analytics tools, no push notifications.
  • Leaderboards only if you are signed in to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center.
  • Personal data is only processed by the services listed below: Google for ads and – only if you buy “Ad-free” – Google Play or the App Store and RevenueCat.

2. Data on your device

The app stores the following only locally in its storage on your device: your height record, which worlds you have reached, the chosen language, your settings (e.g. sound on/off) and whether “Ad-free” is unlocked. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it by clearing the app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

To keep the app free, it shows ads: a short ad break at most after every second game over, never during play. After a game over you can also choose to watch a video to continue with one heart. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

  • Data processed: your device's advertising ID (unless you have disabled it), IP address, device and app information (e.g. model, operating system, language) and interactions with ads.
  • No personalized ads: Because the app is also played by teenagers, we request only non-personalized ads from Google, rated for teens at most. No advertising profiles are created about you or used for ads.
  • Consent: On first launch – where required by law (e.g. in the EU, the UK and Switzerland) – Google's consent dialog asks whether Google may store or access information on your device (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). If you decline, Google only shows limited ads without storing or accessing information on your device. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy settings.
  • iPhone: On first launch, the app uses Apple's “App Tracking Transparency” prompt to ask whether Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), e.g. to measure ads and prevent fraud. Google only receives the advertising ID if you allow it; the ads stay non-personalized even then. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Other purposes at Google: Google also processes data to deliver and bill ads and to prevent fraud (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is funding the free app).
  • Third country: Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

More: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Buying “Ad-free” (in-app purchase)

“Ad-free” is a one-time unlock that removes the ad breaks (the optional videos stay available). The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Only these providers process your payment data – we receive no payment data, only a confirmation of the purchase.

To verify purchases and restore them on a new device, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). This processes a random app user ID, the purchase receipt (product, time, country, currency, price) and the app and operating system version. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on EU Standard Contractual Clauses. More: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

5. Leaderboards (Google Play Games, Apple Game Center)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on Android or Apple Game Center on iPhone, the app sends your record height to the leaderboard after each run. Google or Apple show them together with your player name and profile picture, which other players can see, and process your player ID for this. We do not receive or store this data ourselves. The legal basis is providing the feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Nothing is sent without signing in; you can sign out at any time in the Play Games app or in iOS Settings under Game Center. More: Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy

6. App stores

If you download the app from Google Play or the Apple App Store, these providers process data under their own responsibility (e.g. account, download, crash reports if you have enabled them). Their privacy policies apply.

7. Children and teenagers

The app is intended for players aged 13 and up. There is no account of our own and no chat. Others only see your results in the leaderboards, and only if you are signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center (section 5). All characters and places in the game are fictional. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical details (e.g. browser, time) to deliver the page and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website sets no cookies, loads no external fonts and uses no analytics. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: GitHub Privacy Statement

9. Retention

Data on your device remains until you delete it (see section 2). For data held by Google, RevenueCat, the app stores and GitHub, their retention periods apply.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Write to us at privacy@decly.app.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, which is responsible for us.

11. Changes

If the app changes, we will update this privacy policy. The version published here applies.
