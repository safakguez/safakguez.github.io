Every right answer counts. The rare one wins.

Seven questions a day, the same for everyone – for example “Name a river in the United Kingdom”. You type your answer yourself:

  • Every correct answer scores points.
  • The rarer your answer, the rarer the bird: from sparrow (10 points) to bird-of-paradise (100 points).
  • After each question you see what most people say – and what a real insider tip would have been.

A daily round with a streak, practice rounds, a bird book to fill and a result to share. In 14 languages with local questions for each country, no account.

Free with ads, one-time “Ad-free” purchase. For players aged 13 and up. Answer lists come from Wikidata; rarity is an estimate. Developed with the help of AI (code, text, translations, graphics).
